About this stay

Space, quiet and remarkable views.

Find serenity in a historic Swiss village beside turquoise Lake Brienz.

Brienz Villa on Alpgasse is a stylish home with breathtaking views, ideal for one or two families. It is a five-minute walk to swimming and lakeside restaurants, and approximately 15 minutes to the train and ferry. This stay includes the private main levels of the villa.

The villa has two units. The lower apartment has its own entrance and garden and may be rented separately; groups wanting complete privacy can reserve the entire villa.
