About this stay

Space, quiet and remarkable views.

Find serenity in a historic Swiss village by turquoise Lake Brienz! Brienz Villa on Alpgasse is a stylish home with breathtaking views, ideal for 1 or 2 families. It's a 5 min walk to swimming/lakeside restaurants, 15 min to the train/ferry. Treat yourself to a luxurious vacation and discover the Bernese Oberland from here!

The Villa has 2 units -you rent the main levels of this beautiful home. A lower apartment, with separate entrance/garden, is also rentable.

The space

Feel a warm welcome from a family-owned vacation home!

  • Terrasse with dining area, outdoor fireplace, and spectacular views!
  • Well-equipped kitchen
  • Dining room with lake view
  • Spacious living room with large TV
  • 4 bedrooms (King, King, Queen, and 2 Twin beds)
  • 2 full baths, 1 half bath
  • Washer/dryer (on-site, shared)
  • Fast wifi

  • Parking for up to 3 cars
  • No pets
  • Local quiet hours 10 pm-7 am

Please respect that there is NO SMOKING allowed inside the home.

All the furnishings are new and of good quality. Windows have retractable shutters for privacy.

On the entrance level is the spacious entryway, guest bathroom, kitchen, dining room, and large living room with fireplace. The kitchen is well-equipped (including microwave, raclette, fondue, rice cooker, kettle, Nespresso machine, milk foamer.) From the kitchen and living room you have direct access to the large private patio with a beautiful view of Lake Brienz and surrounding mountains!

The 4 bedrooms are on the upper floor. 1 bathroom with shower, toilet and sink is accessible to all. The other bathroom with corner bath, shower, toilet, double sink is connected to one of the bedrooms w/ King bed. On request we provide a baby bed for toddlers up to 3 years old and a high chair.

The washing machine and dryer are in the basement and is shared with the guests of the lower apartment.

The lower apartment is also rented on Airbnb. It has own private entrance and garden area. If you prefer to rent both units together, please inquire.

Other things to note

Walking time from Brienz train and ferry station about 16 minutes. The path via Feldstrasse has the easiest, most gradual incline with an elevation gain of about 35 meters or 115 feet.

From train stop Brienz West: 12 minutes

As the last part is steep uphill, you might want to take a taxi on your arrival day. A ride for 4 persons and their luggage costs about CHF 30
