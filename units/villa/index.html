About this stay

A home for gathering.

Find serenity in a historic village on the shores of turquoise Lake Brienz in the Swiss Alps.

Brienz Villa on Alpgasse is a stylish home with breathtaking views and is ideal for family gatherings. It is a five-minute walk to swimming spots and restaurants, and approximately 15 minutes to the ferry and train station. Four bedrooms upstairs sleep eight guests; the lower-level apartment has a fifth bedroom and sofa bed for four more.

Renting the entire villa gives your group both self-contained units, two kitchens, separate entrances and generous indoor and outdoor living space.
