About this stay

A home for gathering.

Find Serenity in a historic village on the shores of turquoise Lake Brienz in the Swiss Alps. Brienz Villa on Alpgasse is a stylish home with breathtaking views and is ideal for family gatherings. It's a 5 min walk to swimming spots and restaurants, and 15 min to the ferry/train station. Four bedrooms upstairs sleep 8 persons, and lower level apartment with 5th bedroom and sofa bed sleeps 4 more. Treat yourself to the luxury of a relaxing vacation and discover the Bernese Oberland from here!

Other things to note

Walking time from Brienz train and ferry station about 16 minutes. The path via Feldstrasse has the easiest, most gradual incline with an elevation gain of about 35 meters or 115 feet.

From train stop Brienz West: 12 minutes

As the last part is steep uphill, you might want to take a taxi on your arrival day. A ride for 4 persons and their luggage costs about CHF 30

The next grocery store is Migros. 5 min walking downhill, at main street.

Coop, another grocery store, is located next to Brienz train station.

Along main street you will find more stores and traditional Swiss restaurants, many with seating along the lake.
